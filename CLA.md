Gajae Code App is licensed under the MIT license. This agreement does not
change that. It settles a separate question that the license cannot answer:
**who may decide the project's licensing later.**

Whoever writes a line of code owns the copyright in it. Without an agreement,
every contributor holds a veto over any future licensing decision, and a
contributor who becomes unreachable makes that decision impossible rather than
merely difficult. Sorting this out before a contribution is merged costs a
signature; sorting it out afterwards means finding everyone, or removing their
work.

You keep the copyright in what you write. You are granting rights, not handing
your work over.
